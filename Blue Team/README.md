## 🔎 Investigation Approach

1. **Understand the scenario** — Identify the questions, scope, and supplied evidence.
2. **Preserve and examine artifacts** — Inspect files, logs, captures, or system evidence.
3. **Build a timeline and test hypotheses** — Correlate findings instead of relying on guesses.
4. **Document the result** — Explain what the evidence shows and why.

> All investigations are completed in CTF or authorized lab environments.
